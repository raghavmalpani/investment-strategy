# CRDO - Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd

## Company Overview
- **Sector:** Semiconductors / Communications Equipment
- **Market Cap:** $25B
- **Employees:** 622
- **Exchange:** NASDAQ

**Business:** High-speed connectivity solutions for data centers — SerDes IP, Active Electrical Cables (AECs), line card retimers. Critical for GPU-to-GPU fabric in AI clusters. Direct competitor to Astera Labs (ALAB).
