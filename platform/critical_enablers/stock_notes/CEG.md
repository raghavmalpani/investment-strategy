### Key Points

**AI Revenue Growth (4/5):**
- **Direct AI exposure:** Microsoft 20-year deal to restart Three Mile Island for datacenter power (announced Sept 2024)
- Nuclear capacity increasingly dedicated to AI/datacenter loads - hyperscalers seeking carbon-free baseload power
- Revenue growth 2.6% YoY (Q2 2025), but AI-specific contracts not fully reflected yet
- Pipeline: Hyperscalers (GOOGL, AMZN, META) all exploring nuclear power agreements
- **Why 4/5:** Clear AI datacenter monetization path, deals announced but revenue ramp still early

**Competitive Positioning (5/5):**
- **Largest nuclear fleet in US:** 21 reactors, ~20% of US nuclear capacity
- Only pure-play nuclear power generator at scale in US
- Carbon-free baseload: AI datacenters need 24/7 reliable power, nuclear is only carbon-free option at scale (solar/wind intermittent)
- Regulatory expertise: Decades operating nuclear, can navigate NRC approvals for restarts/extensions
- **Best-in-class:** No competitor has comparable nuclear scale + datacenter power positioning

**Strategic Moats (4/5):**
- Asset moat: Nuclear plants take 10+ years to build, existing fleet irreplaceable
- Long-term contracts: 20-year Microsoft deal locks in revenue, other hyperscalers likely follow
- Regulatory moat: New nuclear extremely difficult to permit, existing reactors have massive advantage
- **Not 5/5:** Power purchase agreements can be renegotiated, some regulatory risk on reactor life extensions

**Execution & Regulatory Risk (3/5):**
- **Execution solid:** Three Mile Island restart on track, strong operational history
- Regulatory: NRC oversight, reactor life extension approvals required, environmental reviews
- Customer concentration: Microsoft deal is huge but creates dependence on single hyperscaler for growth
- Nuclear safety: Any major incident industry-wide could impact sentiment/policy

**Capital Efficiency (4/5):**
- ROE 22.3%, ROIC 17.7% - strong returns for utility
- Gross margin 19.3%, operating margin 17.8% - healthy for power generation
- FCF currently negative (-$2.5B) due to Three Mile Island restart capex, but should turn positive post-2026
- Interest coverage 8.8x, manageable debt (D/E 0.62)
- **Why 4/5:** Good returns but near-term FCF negative due to restart capex

**Valuation (2/5):**
- P/E 39.6x - expensive for utility, pricing in AI datacenter growth
- P/S 4.8x, EV/EBITDA 17.8x - premium to traditional utilities
- Market cap $119B - AI datacenter thesis heavily priced in
- **Why 2/5:** Limited upside unless more hyperscaler deals announced
