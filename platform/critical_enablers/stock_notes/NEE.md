### Key Points

**AI Revenue Growth (3/5):**
- Indirect AI datacenter exposure via renewable + grid infrastructure
- Revenue growth 2.5% YoY (Q2 2025) - steady utility growth but no AI breakout
- No disclosed AI datacenter-specific contracts like CEG/Microsoft deal
- Renewable capacity expansion benefits datacenter demand indirectly (solar/wind for grid-scale power)
- Florida Power & Light (FPL) + NextEra Energy Resources (NEER) dual model - both relevant to AI infrastructure buildout
- **Why 3/5:** Solid infrastructure provider but no direct AI monetization visibility, unlike CEG

**Competitive Positioning (4/5):**
- **World's largest renewable energy producer:** 30+ GW wind/solar capacity via NEER
- Dominant in Florida utility market via FPL (10M+ customers)
- Grid-scale battery storage leader - critical for datacenter reliability when paired with renewables
- Renewable + storage combo addresses AI datacenter carbon-free power needs (solar/wind + batteries for 24/7 reliability)
- **Not 5/5:** Renewables are intermittent (unlike nuclear's baseload), requires storage/grid integration complexity

**Strategic Moats (4/5):**
- Regulated utility moat: FPL has geographic monopoly in Florida, rate recovery mechanisms
- Scale advantage: Largest renewable operator, can deploy capital efficiently
- Long-term PPAs: Multi-year renewable power purchase agreements with corporates (not AI-specific but sticky)
- Battery storage leadership creates differentiation vs pure wind/solar
- **Not 5/5:** Renewables more commoditized than nuclear, easier for competitors to replicate

**Execution & Regulatory Risk (4/5):**
- **Execution solid:** Industry-leading operational track record, consistent capex deployment
- Regulatory: Utility regulation (Florida PSC) generally favorable, renewable tax credits supportive
- Geographic diversification: FPL (Florida utility) + NEER (nationwide renewables) reduces concentration
- No customer concentration risk - diversified utility + wholesale power
- Climate risk: Hurricane exposure in Florida (physical infrastructure risk)
- **Why 4/5:** Manageable regulatory environment, proven execution, but hurricane/climate risks

**Capital Efficiency (3/5):**
- ROIC 5.1%, ROE 11.6% - acceptable for utility but below CEG
- Gross margin 62%, operating margin 35.7% - healthy for regulated utility
- FCF positive but thin ($3.7B FCF, 2.2% yield) - capex-heavy renewables buildout
- Interest coverage 2.4x - leveraged balance sheet (D/E 1.83)
- **Why 3/5:** Utility-level returns, capital-intensive, financial quality adequate but not exceptional

**Valuation (2/5):**
- P/E 29.3x - premium for utility, pricing in renewable growth story
- P/S 6.7x, EV/EBITDA 17.4x - expensive vs traditional utilities
- Market cap $174B - large-cap utility, AI datacenter thesis partially reflected
- **Why 2/5:** High premium vs utility peers, limited upside unless direct AI deals announced
