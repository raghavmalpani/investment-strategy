### Scoring Rationale
- **AI Revenue (5/5):** Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) continues strong; Q3 FY26 $27B revenue with AI server segment driving growth; backlog conversion accelerating; >40% AI-related growth in server segment
- **Competitive Positioning (3/5):** Revised down — Dell assembles/integrates components from NVIDIA, Micron, Broadcom without producing scarce proprietary components; scale and service network are advantages but don't create pricing power; competitors (SMCI, CLS, HPE) can substitute

- **Execution Risk (4/5):** Diversified customer base reduces concentration risk; GPU allocation improving; solid execution on backlog conversion
- **Capital Efficiency (5/5):** ROIC 30.5% exceptional; FCF yield 5.2% healthy; strong cash generation despite aggressive investment
- **Valuation (4/5):** P/E 16x, P/S 0.83x = attractive vs growth profile; significant upside if AI server demand sustains; thesis NOT fully priced

### Key Points
- Revenue volatility between quarters reflects AI server shipment timing (large backlog conversions)

- Valuation compressed since last review (P/E 19x -> 16x) while fundamentals improved
