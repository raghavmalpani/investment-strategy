**Strategic Moats (3/5):**
- Hyperscaler partnerships: Multi-year production agreements, switching costs moderate (12-18 months to qualify new ODM)
- Operational excellence: On-time delivery, quality track record creates stickiness
- Geographic footprint: North American manufacturing (Mexico, Canada) = supply chain diversification vs pure Asia exposure
- **Not 4/5:** Contract manufacturing is ultimately commoditizable, pricing pressure from hyperscaler negotiations

- **Execution excellent:** Revenue and profitability accelerating, no major production delays
- Customer concentration: Likely >50% from top 3-5 hyperscalers (not fully disclosed)
- Geopolitical: Canada-based with Mexico/Asia manufacturing reduces single-country risk
- Supply chain: Component sourcing (GPUs, NICs) depends on NVDA/Broadcom allocation
- **Why 4/5:** Proven execution, manageable concentration risk, supply chain dependency moderate
