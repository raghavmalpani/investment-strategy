### Key Points

**AI Revenue Growth (5/5):**

- **Why 5/5:** >40% growth threshold crushed, 98% YoY with expanding TAM as AI clusters scale

**Competitive Positioning (4/5):**
- **PCIe/CXL leadership:** Dominant in high-speed interconnect chips for AI servers (retimers enable signal integrity at PCIe 5.0/6.0 speeds)
- Technology lead: First to market with PCIe 6.0 retimers, CXL 2.0/3.0 memory expansion controllers
- Design wins at all major hyperscalers + ODMs (Foxconn, Wistron, Inventec)
- Competitors: Rambus (PCIe retimers), Montage Technology (CXL), but ALAB has first-mover advantage
- **Why 4/5:** Strong differentiation in niche but dependent on continued AI server scaling, not vertically integrated like NVDA

**Strategic Moats (3/5):**
- Design-in stickiness: Once designed into server platform, multi-year production runs (switching costs high)
- Technology lead: 12-18 month advantage on PCIe 6.0 and CXL 3.0 creates temporary moat
- Partnership with NVDA: Co-optimized for H100/H200/B200 systems increases switching costs
- **Not 4/5:** Retimer/controller chips are eventually commoditizable, moat erodes as competitors catch up on technology

**Execution & Regulatory Risk (3/5):**
- **Execution solid:** Revenue and profitability inflection achieved (losses → profits in 2025)
- Customer concentration: Likely high exposure to top 3 hyperscalers (MSFT/META/GOOGL) via ODMs, but not disclosed
- Supply chain: Fabless model (TSMC manufactured) reduces capital intensity but creates foundry dependency
- No major regulatory risks (US-based, no China export control exposure disclosed)
- **Why 3/5:** Proven execution but customer concentration and foundry dependency are moderate risks

**Capital Efficiency (4/5):**

- ROE 8.8%, ROA 7.9% - healthy for semiconductor startup
- FCF $210M (up 130% YoY), FCF yield 0.6% - positive momentum

- Balance sheet fortress: $162M cash, zero debt, current ratio 11x
- **Why 4/5:** Strong profitability and FCF, ROIC not disclosed but implied to be healthy
