# Commerce & Ads Scorecard

## Segment Overview
Commerce and advertising platforms monetize user intent and transaction data. AI enhances targeting precision, conversion optimization, and personalization. The key driver is **first-party data moats** - companies with closed-loop attribution (search → ad → purchase) compound their advantage as AI models improve, creating winner-take-most dynamics.

**Why this matters:** Amazon knows what you searched, clicked, and bought. Google/Meta know your identity graph across devices. Shopify aggregates commerce data across millions of merchants. These proprietary datasets cannot be replicated, and AI makes them exponentially more valuable for targeting and conversion.

## Risk Profile
**This is a high-growth, high-risk sleeve.** Scoring emphasizes first-party data depth and AI-addressable conversion upside over current ad revenue. We tolerate privacy regulation risk if the data moat is durable.

## Universal Scoring Dimensions (Total Weight: 7.5)

### 1. First-Party Data Moats (Weight: 2.0)
**What we're measuring:** Proprietary data that competitors cannot access or replicate

**Sample Indicators:**
- **Closed-loop attribution:** Can they measure search → ad click → transaction? (Amazon yes, display-only ad networks no)
- **Identity graph scale:** Logged-in users, cross-device tracking, persistent IDs immune to cookie deprecation
- **Transaction data depth:** Purchase history, basket composition, repeat behavior (not just clicks/impressions)
- **Proprietary behavioral graphs:** Social graph (Meta), shopping graph (Amazon), merchant + buyer graph (Shopify)
- **Data freshness:** Real-time transaction signals vs batch-updated data

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Full closed-loop attribution + identity graph + transaction data at scale (Amazon, Meta logged-in users, Google Search + Shopping)
- **4:** Strong identity graph + some transaction visibility OR deep transaction data with limited cross-platform tracking (Shopify, MercadoLibre)
- **3:** Good behavioral data but incomplete attribution loop OR limited logged-in penetration
- **2:** Partial data moats, vulnerable to cookie deprecation or platform changes
- **1:** Commodity data sources, no proprietary advantage

### 2. AI-Addressable Conversion & Monetization Upside (Weight: 2.0)
**What we're measuring:** How much can AI improve ad ROAS, conversion rates, and pricing power?

**Sample Indicators:**
- **Conversion rate headroom:** Current e-commerce conversion rates (2-4% typical) → AI-driven personalization upside (conversational commerce, hyper-personalized recommendations)
- **Ad ROAS improvement potential:** Can AI targeting drive measurable advertiser ROI gains? (Better creative, audience precision, bid optimization)
- **Take-rate expansion:** Can AI justify higher merchant fees or ad prices? (Shopify charging for AI merchandising tools, Amazon increasing referral fees)
- **Fulfillment automation leverage:** Warehouse AI, delivery route optimization reducing cost-to-serve
- **New ad formats:** AI-generated creative, conversational shopping, voice commerce unlocking incremental inventory

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Massive conversion upside (voice/agent commerce unlocking), clear ROAS improvements, take-rate pricing power, automation leverage visible
- **4:** Strong conversion headroom, ROAS gains measurable, some take-rate expansion or cost reduction
- **3:** Moderate improvements, competitive with peers, incremental gains
- **2:** Limited upside, AI features not driving measurable lift
- **1:** No clear AI monetization path

### 3. Strategic Moats (Weight: 1.5)
**What we're measuring:** Durable competitive advantages beyond data and AI

**Sample Indicators:**
- **Platform lock-in:** Merchant switching costs (Shopify store builds, Amazon FBA logistics), advertiser API integrations
- **Network effects:** Two-sided marketplace liquidity (more buyers → more sellers → better selection)
- **Fulfillment/logistics infrastructure:** Physical assets creating barriers (Amazon warehouses, MercadoLibre delivery network)
- **Brand/trust moats:** Consumer trust in shopping experience, fraud prevention reputation

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Multiple reinforcing moats - marketplace network effects + logistics infrastructure + data (Amazon)
- **4:** Strong two-sided network effects + meaningful switching costs (Shopify ecosystem, MercadoLibre LatAm dominance)
- **3:** Moderate lock-in, some network effects, manageable competition
- **2:** Weak moats, commoditized offerings
- **1:** No defensible position

### 4. Execution & Regulatory Risk (Weight: 1.0)
**What we're measuring:** Privacy regulation, antitrust risk, and execution on AI products

**Sample Indicators:**
- **Privacy regulation exposure:** GDPR, cookie deprecation impact, third-party data restrictions
- **Antitrust scrutiny:** FTC/EU cases on marketplace self-preferencing, ad targeting monopolies
- **AI product velocity:** Shipping pace, advertiser/merchant adoption of AI tools
- **Platform policy risk:** Dependence on iOS/Android (Meta/Snap) vs owned ecosystem (Amazon)

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Minimal regulatory risk, strong AI execution, platform independence
- **4:** Manageable regulation, good AI velocity, some platform dependence
- **3:** Moderate regulatory headwinds, mixed AI adoption
- **2:** Active antitrust cases or slow AI execution
- **1:** Existential regulatory threat or broken execution

### 5. Capital Efficiency (Weight: 0.5)
**What we're measuring:** Quality filter - can they fund AI and infrastructure buildout?

**Sample Indicators:**
- Free cash flow generation
- Gross margin stability (e-commerce typically 20-40%, ads 50-80%)
- Capex intensity (fulfillment centers, ad targeting infrastructure)
- Path to profitability for AI features

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** High-margin cash machine (Google/Meta ads business)
- **4:** Profitable with good cash flow, can fund AI investments
- **3:** Breakeven or low margins but improving (Shopify, MercadoLibre)
- **2:** Burning cash but path to profitability visible
- **1:** Distressed financials, unsustainable burn

### 6. Valuation as Thesis Discount (Weight: 0.5)
**What we're measuring:** How much upside remains if AI drives conversion/ROAS gains?

**Sample Indicators:**
- Current valuation vs AI-driven GMV/ad revenue potential
- Market pricing in AI-driven take-rate expansion?
- Optionality value: Conversational commerce, new ad formats not in model

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Significant upside if AI conversion gains realized, market skeptical
- **4:** Reasonable upside, some AI expectations in price
- **3:** Fair value, market pricing moderate AI success
- **2:** Full value, requires flawless execution
- **1:** Overvalued, market pricing perfection

**Trigger Flags (no score)** | — | Major privacy regulation forcing data restrictions, antitrust breakup orders, advertiser exodus due to ROI collapse, platform policy changes killing distribution (iOS blocking Meta tracking)
