### Key Points

**Platform Control (5/5):**
- Full vertical integration: owns hardware (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch, Vision Pro) + OS (iOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS)
- App Store tax model enables 30% cut on all AI agent transactions
- Controls defaults for voice/search/agents - Apple Intelligence is OS-level, gatekeeper for all AI distribution
- Platform independence: OpenAI/Google partnerships are strategic, but Apple controls the OS layer

**Installed Base (5/5):**
- ~2 billion active devices globally (largest AI-capable footprint)
- Premium demographic: high-income users willing to pay for AI features
- iPhone 17 series + M4 Macs driving AI hardware refresh with on-device NPU requirements
- Strong upgrade velocity: Q3 2025 shows accelerating PC/iPad refresh for "AI-ready" devices

**Agent Readiness (4/5):**
- Apple Intelligence launched WWDC 2025: systemwide AI (mail, search, writing, image gen, context-aware)
- On-device Neural Engine (iPhone 17, M4) enables privacy + low-latency AI
- Siri overhaul in progress: ChatGPT + Gemini integrations show openness to best models while maintaining OS control
- Third-party LLM API access now available - major step toward becoming default AI distribution layer
- **Not 5/5 yet:** Still catching up to Google's Gemini integration depth across Android

**Regulatory Risk (3/5):**
- EU DMA compliance forcing side-loading and alternative app stores
- DOJ/antitrust scrutiny on default search deals and App Store monopoly
- Privacy execution remains strong (on-device AI, federated learning)
- Track record on platform transitions is excellent (Intel’Apple Silicon, USB-C)
- Geographic fragmentation: EU forcing openness, US/China have different regulatory approaches

**Financial Quality (5/5):**
- FCF >$20B/quarter - cash machine
- Gross margin 46.7%, operating margin 31.7%
- ROE 150%, ROIC 78% - exceptional capital efficiency
- Can absorb massive AI infrastructure investments without financial stress
- Q3 2025: $94B revenue, $23.4B net income, $3.66T market cap
