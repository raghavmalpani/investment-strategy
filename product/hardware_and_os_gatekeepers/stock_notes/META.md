### Key Points

**Platform Control (4/5):**
- Owns Quest VR headsets (Quest 2/3/3S) + Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses with full OS control (Horizon OS, custom glasses OS)
- Can set default agents and tax transactions inside Quest Store and Horizon Worlds
- **Building platform independence:** Defensive response to iOS ATT killing $10B ad revenue - needs own hardware layer
- Horizon OS opened to select third parties but Meta controls defaults and distribution
- **Not 5/5:** Install base tiny vs iOS/Android, Quest Store monetization limited to VR content, Ray-Ban lacks app store model yet

**Installed Base (2/5):**
- Quest headsets: 20M+ total sales, 77-90% VR market share but **declining 2025 sales**
- Ray-Ban Meta: **Momentum shifting here** - sales tripled YoY, AI glasses now leading Reality Labs revenue driver
- Quest 4 canceled, next-gen ultralight device delayed to 2026 due to weak consumer VR demand
- Install base quality: innovative early adopters but less affluent than Apple, lower willingness to pay
- Quest Store content revenue: $2.9B by March 2025 shows some monetization, but far behind mobile app stores
- **Major weakness:** Tiny footprint vs 2B+ iPhone, 3B+ Android devices

**Agent Readiness (3/5):**
- Ray-Ban Meta glasses: multimodal AI assistant (vision, voice, contextual search) with rising usage, supply-constrained due to demand
- Quest: voice agent controls improving but "killer use cases" still emerging, limited standalone agent monetization
- Developer API maturity for Quest/Ray-Ban exists but lags iOS/Android for third-party AI agent distribution
- On-device AI silicon at earliest stage vs Apple/Google NPUs
- **Not 4/5+:** Agent ecosystem immature, no clear distribution advantage vs established platforms yet

**Regulatory Risk (2/5):**
- **Major execution risk:** Reality Labs losing $4.5B/quarter (Q2 2025), management expects continued unprofitability
- Pivot from VR headsets ’ AI glasses happening but execution uncertain, losses accelerating
- Regulatory pressure rising as Meta becomes AR/VR "gatekeeper" but still lower scrutiny than Apple/Google (smaller impact)
- Privacy issues with cameras/microphones in smart glasses under constant review
- **High risk:** Betting billions on unproven AR/spatial computing market, no guarantee of consumer adoption

**Financial Quality (4/5):**
- Q2 2025: $47.5B revenue (+12.3% YoY), $18.3B net income from core advertising business
- Gross margin 82.1%, operating margin 44.9% on core business
- Reality Labs revenue: $370M/quarter (~1% of total) but **negative margins**, burning cash
- Total company: ROE 36.7%, strong FCF generation from ads subsidizes hardware losses
- **Not 5/5:** Reality Labs losses are massive and accelerating, requires core ad business to remain strong to fund AR/VR bet
