# Hardware/Endpoint Gatekeepers Scorecard

## Segment Overview
Hardware endpoint gatekeepers own the devices and operating systems through which users access AI services. These are **call options on UI paradigm shifts** - betting that if interaction models change (voice-first, agent-first, AR overlays), OS-level control translates to the ability to tax, disintermediate, or set defaults for AI agent interactions.

**Why this matters:** If Apple can make Siri the default voice assistant, or Meta can make Quest the default AR platform, they can capture value even if their AI models are mediocre - because they control the entry point.

## Risk Profile
**This is a high-growth, high-risk sleeve.** Scoring emphasizes future platform leverage over current AI revenue, as these companies hold optionality on taxing/disintermediating existing ecosystems when AI interaction models shift.

## Universal Scoring Dimensions (Total Weight: 7.5)

### 1. Platform Control & Independence (Weight: 2.5)
**What we're measuring:** OS/hardware ownership that provides leverage over AI distribution

**Sample Indicators:**
- **Hardware + OS ownership:** Do they manufacture devices and control the OS?
- **Default positioning power:** Can they set defaults for voice/search/agents?
- **Ecosystem tax capability:** Can they take a cut of AI transactions (App Store model)?
- **Platform independence:** Are they dependent on another company's platform?

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Own hardware + OS + can tax ecosystem (iOS, Android if Google made Pixel dominant)
- **4:** Own OS but limited hardware (Android fragmentation) OR own hardware building new platform (Meta Quest/Ray-Ban)
- **3:** Strong ecosystem but platform-dependent (Windows on ARM, declining PC market share)
- **2:** Niche hardware or weak OS control
- **1:** No platform ownership, pure software play

### 2. Installed Base & Device Refresh (Weight: 2.0)
**What we're measuring:** Distribution reach when they flip the AI switch

**Sample Indicators:**
- Active device install base with AI-capable silicon
- Device refresh velocity and upgrade penetration rates
- User cohort quality (spending power, willingness to pay for AI features)
- Geographic reach and regulatory fragmentation

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** >500M AI-capable devices, premium cohort, rapid refresh (iPhone)
- **4:** 100-500M devices or strong upgrade momentum (Android flagship users, Quest ecosystem)
- **3:** 50-100M devices or slower refresh cycles (Windows PCs with NPUs)
- **2:** <50M devices but growing quickly (Ray-Ban Meta, new form factors)
- **1:** Minimal installed base or declining penetration

### 3. Agent/Voice Integration Readiness (Weight: 1.5)
**What we're measuring:** How close are they to monetizing AI distribution?

**Sample Indicators:**
- On-device NPU/AI silicon penetration (A17, M4, Snapdragon X)
- Voice assistant/agent quality trajectory
- Strategic model partnerships (Apple × OpenAI, GM × MSFT)
- Developer API maturity (App Intents, Android Agent APIs)
- Early product launches (Apple Intelligence, Copilot+ PCs, FSD)

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** AI silicon deployed, quality agents shipping, strong partnerships
- **4:** AI silicon rolling out, partnerships announced, demos promising
- **3:** Roadmap visible, early pilots, partnerships forming
- **2:** Experimenting but no clear strategy
- **1:** No credible AI agent strategy

### 4. Execution & Regulatory Risk (Weight: 1.0)
**What we're measuring:** Can they execute the platform pivot without breaking the business?

**Sample Indicators:**
- Antitrust/regulatory scrutiny (App Store monopoly cases, forced interoperability)
- Safety/quality track record (vehicle recalls, privacy breaches)
- Management execution history on platform shifts
- Geographic policy fragmentation (EU vs US vs China)

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Clean regulatory record, proven execution on transitions
- **4:** Manageable regulatory risk, solid execution
- **3:** Moderate antitrust pressure or execution concerns
- **2:** Active antitrust cases or repeated execution failures
- **1:** Existential regulatory threat or broken execution

### 5. Financial Quality (Weight: 0.5)
**What we're measuring:** Can they fund the AI infrastructure buildout?

**Sample Indicators:**
- Free cash flow generation
- Gross margin stability
- R&D investment capacity
- Balance sheet strength for hardware losses

**Scoring guide:**
- **5:** Cash machine, can fund aggressive hardware buildout (Apple, Meta)
- **4:** Strong FCF, comfortable investing in AI
- **3:** Breakeven but limited investment capacity
- **2:** Burning cash but path to profitability visible
- **1:** Distressed financials, can't fund transition

**Trigger Flags (no score)** | — | Forced API openness (EU DMA), major safety recalls, privacy scandals causing user exodus, failed platform transitions
